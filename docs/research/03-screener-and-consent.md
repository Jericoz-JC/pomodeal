# Screener and Consent

## Recruiting message (paste into a group chat)

> Hey! A few of us are building a study app for people who study a lot (and get pulled onto their phones). We're looking for 6–8 people to talk with for 45 min this week or next: some questions about how you study, then trying a rough prototype on your phone. Coffee or a $10 gift card on us. If you're up for it, fill this out (2 min): [form link]

## Screener form (Google Form or Tally, 6 questions)

1. **Are you 18 or older and currently enrolled in college or grad school?** Yes / No → *No ends the form: "Thanks! We're only talking with students 18+ right now."*
2. **What are you studying, and what year are you?** (short text)
3. **On a typical week, how many nights do you study on your own?** 0–1 / 2 / 3–4 / 5+ → *Prefer 3+*
4. **When you study, where is your phone usually?** In my hand or on the desk / In my bag or another room / It depends
5. **Have you ever used an app or method to help you study (timer, blocker, Pomodoro, Forest, etc.)?** Yes and still use it / Yes but stopped / No → *Aim for a mix: at least 2 "stopped" and 2 "no"*
6. **Which times work for a 45-min chat?** (checkboxes for the Nov 11–18 slots) + **Best way to reach you** (phone or Instagram handle)

Don't ask about diagnoses, medication, grades or GPA.

## Consent (read aloud at the start, note "yes" with the time)

> Before we start: this is a 45-minute conversation for a student project called Pomodeal. We'll ask how you study, then you'll try a prototype. I'll take notes with your first name only. [If recording:] I'd also like to record audio so we don't miss anything. Only our team hears it, and we delete it once we've written up our notes, within 2 weeks. You can skip any question, stop at any time, or ask us to delete your notes later, just text me. The thank-you is yours either way. Is that OK? [And is recording OK?]

If they say anything about a diagnosis, medication or health: don't write it down, and don't ask follow-ups. Gently steer back to what happened that night.

## Data handling

- Notes live in `docs/research/sessions/` as `P01.md`, `P02.md`… with first names only. If the repo becomes shared or public, move them out first. **Don't commit them to a public repo.**
- Audio goes in a private folder outside the repo and is deleted after synthesis (by Dec 2, 2026).
- Participants can ask for deletion any time; delete their notes file and audio and note it in the synthesis.
