# Settled decisions

Chris's rulings. Give this file to every reviewer. Reviewers may say a decision looks wrong, but nobody changes one
without asking Chris first.

## Who it's for

- Pomodeal is built for Chris's taste first: a capable student using it nightly. Not an accessibility or health product.
- Fast and intuitive beats explained. **No copy that explains basic UI** ("Tap an underlined word…", "Drag the line…",
  "One tap."). Users get it.
- No keyboard-key badges printed on controls. Shortcuts work silently.

## Look

- One "approved" green on grey-green paper. Archivo (two widths: display and action) for numbers, headings and anything
  you press; Atkinson Hyperlegible for sentences.
- Banned: indigo or purple with yellow, all-caps eyebrow labels, `·` separators, monospace data labels, identical rounded
  cards, sawtooth or triangle edges.
- Torn paper shows round half-holes, never points.
- Focus is a solid ring, a break is a hatched ring. A running block is the dial alone on the desk: no card, no DEAL stamp, no label, one tick at 12, a small "min".
- Green is wet ink: the desk is dull, the paper is the bright thing, green appears where a stamp lands or time is paid. At rest DEAL is a solid green die.
- Dark mode: a near-black desk with pale paper and dark ink on tickets, sheets, the tape and cards.

## The deal

- The deal is a sentence on a paper ticket: "10 min of Reels, then 8 min of BIO 180 ch. 7." The words you can change are
  underlined. It is not a form and not a list of line items (tried and rejected).
- Anything that commits you is a stamp (DEAL, Start reading, Save pieces). Declining is a paper button. A link is the
  quiet way out.
- A deal is one sitting and ends on the done sheet (spec 010).
- "Needs more than one sitting?" is a quiet line under the ticket, not on it. The clock answers in words, with no small wheel.
- The screen stays on during every focus block; there is no switch.

## Choosing time

- People pick a time from choices, the way the deal's length picker works. No − and + steppers, no number fields.
- A big one's pieces are lengths of one ticket; you drag a tear line to change one (spec 009, round 7).

## Process

- Every visible change goes to the design council in small rounds before it is committed.
- A reviewer's fix is checked against this file and against how the app already does the same job, before it is built.
